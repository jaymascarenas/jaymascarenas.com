About

I build software, and the teams that build it.

I've spent more than 15 years designing, building and shipping web applications with thoughtful UI/UX, almost always as part of a collaborative team. I like the whole arc of it: talking to the people who'll use a product, sketching the architecture, writing the code and seeing it hold up in production.

As a Director of Engineering, I lead teams that build internal and customer-facing applications that move the business forward and make people more productive. I care about clear goals, honest feedback and giving engineers room to do their best work.

Before that I taught full-stack web development at a coding bootcamp, which is still one of the most rewarding things I've done. These days I also run Black Rim Solutions LLC, where I build custom software, iOS apps and AI-powered workflows. I build with AI every day and keep learning the newest tools in the space.
